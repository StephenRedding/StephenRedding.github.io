NBER International Trade and Geography Data

This web page contains links to international trade data compiled by the National Bureau of Economic Research (NBER). Most of these data were compiled by Robert Feenstra and/or Peter Schott and further details about the data can be obtained from their websites.

These data are intended for educational and research purposes. Your use of the data indicates agreement with these terms of use.
